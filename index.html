How it works

From your sentence set
to scored data.

01

Administer or upload your EIT

Load your sentence set and run the task here on the schedule of the original design, or upload recordings you have already collected. Any number of items; the 30-item English EIT of Ortega et al. (2002) is included as a preset, with the stimulus recordings of Park (under 2nd review).

02

Transcribed by Whisper

Whisper large-v3-turbo transcribes every recording in the browser, so nobody types out what the participants said. Any transcription can still be corrected by hand before it is scored.

03

Every item scored automatically

Each transcription is aligned against your target word by word, and word error rate (WER) and accuracy are calculated for you. The normalization rules behind them stay visible, editable, and exported with the results.

Privacy by architecture

Participant audio never
leaves the computer.

Speech recognition runs inside the browser itself. There is no server to send recordings to, because there is no server.

No upload, no account

Nothing to sign up for and nothing transmitted. Open the page and work — there is no account to create and no usage limit to reach.

Runs on your GPU

Whisper large-v3-turbo runs via WebGPU where available, with a WASM fallback. The model is downloaded once (about 1.5 GB) and cached, after which a 30-item set takes roughly five to ten minutes on a recent laptop.

Easier ethics approval

Because identifiable speech data stay on the researcher's own machine, no third-party processor needs to be declared.
